# Executive Summary

## Project
Teen Phone Addiction Analysis

## Business Question
How does daily mobile phone usage affect students' academic performance?

## Findings
- Students with higher daily phone usage generally show lower academic performance.
- Increased phone usage is associated with reduced sleep duration.
- Students with balanced phone usage tend to have better academic performance.

## Recommendations
1. Encourage students to limit daily phone usage.
2. Promote healthy sleep habits by reducing phone usage before bedtime.
3. Monitor screen time and educate students on responsible mobile phone usage.
