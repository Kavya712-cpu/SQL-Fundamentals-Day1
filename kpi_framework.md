# KPI Framework

| KPI                          | Formula                       | Owner     | Frequency | Target   | Type    |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------- | --------- | --------- | -------- | ------- |
| Average Academic Performance | Average(Academic_Performance) | Principal | Monthly   | >80      | Lagging |
| Average Screen Time          | Average(Daily_Screen_Time)    | School    | Weekly    | <4 Hours | Leading |
| Total Students               | CountRows()                   | Admin     | Monthly   | Monitor  | Lagging |
| Average Sleep Hours          | Average(Sleep_Hours)          | School    | Weekly    | >8 Hours | Leading |
| Attendance Rate              | Attendance %                  | Teacher   | Daily     | >90%     | Leading |
| Assignment Completion        | Completed/Total               | Teacher   | Weekly    | >95%     | Leading |
| Pass Percentage              | Passed/Total                  | Principal | Monthly   | >90%     | Lagging |
| Average Study Hours          | Average(Study_Hours)          | Student   | Weekly    | >3 Hours | Leading |
| High Screen Time Students    | Count(Screen Time >5 hrs)     | School    | Weekly    | <20%     | Leading |
| Healthy Sleep Students       | Count(Sleep >8 hrs)           | School    | Weekly    | >80%     | Leading |

# KPI Narrative

Students with higher screen time tend to have lower academic performance. Monitoring screen time, sleep hours, and study habits helps improve student performance. The dashboard provides key insights for better decision-making.
