# AutoViz vs SweetViz Comparison

## AutoViz

- Automatically generates multiple visualizations.
- Best for detailed exploratory data analysis.
- Suitable for data analysts.
- Produces various statistical charts.

## SweetViz

- Generates an interactive HTML report.
- Easy to understand and visually attractive.
- Best for business stakeholders.
- Includes feature summaries, missing values, and correlations.

## Recommendation

I recommend SweetViz for business stakeholders because it provides a clean, interactive, and easy-to-read HTML report. AutoViz is more suitable for analysts performing detailed exploratory data analysis.

## Business Insights from Automated EDA

1. The automated EDA reports helped identify the distribution of numerical and categorical variables quickly, reducing manual analysis time.

2. Correlation analysis helped identify relationships between variables, which can support better feature selection and business decision-making.

3. The reports highlighted missing values and data quality issues, showing the importance of data cleaning before further analysis.
